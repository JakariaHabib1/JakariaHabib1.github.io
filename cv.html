  • Undergraduate Research Thesis: “Physics-Based Inverse Modeling for Sub-Millisecond Neural Delay Estimation in Human Locomotion” (Advisor: Md. Imran Hossain, Faculty, Dept. of ICE, PUST).
    Formulated an end-to-end OpenSim pipeline inverting Hill-type activation ODEs across 45,445 synthetic gait trials, estimating latent delays with MAE = 0.10 ms and R² = 0.998.
