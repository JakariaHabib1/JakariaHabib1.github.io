Jakaria Habib

Embodied AI · Action-Conditioned World Models · Foundation Models · Physics-Informed ML

I am a final-year undergraduate researcher in Information & Communication Engineering at Pabna University of Science & Technology (PUST). My research investigates how autonomous systems perceive, model, and reliably interact with continuous physical environments.

My core research investigates three primary directions:

  • Embodied Intelligence & World Models: Formulating action-conditioned generative representations and certifying trust horizons for safe robotic decision-making and control.
  • Learning Dynamics & Foundation Models: Continual reinforcement learning to mitigate specialist drift during post-training, alongside robust meta-faithfulness verification of multi-step chain-of-thought reasoning.
  • Physics-Informed Perception & Neuromechanics: Interfacing physical musculoskeletal solvers (Stanford OpenSim) with machine learning to model latent neuromuscular delays (R² > 0.98 over 45k synthetic gait trials).

My work includes a First-Author SCI/Scopus Q1 publication in Springer Nature's Earth Science Informatics (IF: 4.2), accepted proceedings at NeurIPS 2026 (NeurReps Track, PMLR) and NeurIPS 2026 (Robotics World Modeling Workshop), and manuscripts under review at ICLR 2027. I am an elected professional member of the American Physical Society (APS #62251156) and an oral speaker at IEEE QPAIN 2025.

I am actively preparing for prospective graduate research studies (Ph.D. / M.S.) in Computer Science, Machine Learning, and Robotics starting in 2026/2027.

Research Interests & Focus

Embodied AI & Robotics

Action-conditioned world models, trust horizon verification for decision-making agents, real-time edge vision (YOLOv8), and autonomous robotic navigation under environmental uncertainty.

Foundation Models & Reasoning

Continual reinforcement learning to mitigate specialist drift in foundation models, meta-faithfulness verification of multi-step chain-of-thought reasoning, and autonomous multi-agent consensus frameworks.

Physics-Informed ML & Bio-Perception

Inverse neuromechanical parameter estimation via Stanford OpenSim, physiological signal processing (PPG/HRV), and interpretable Vision Transformer architectures for high-resolution satellite imagery.
