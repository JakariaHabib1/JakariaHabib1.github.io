The Symmetry Budget: How Much Group Structure Should a Network Architecture Assume?

2026
Jakaria Habib, Noor Islam S. Mohammad, Mahmudul Hasan, Md. Basim Al Zabir Shammo, Hasan Siddiki
NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Symmetry and Geometry in Neural Representations (NeurReps Proceedings Track, to appear in PMLR)

Derives an exact analytical theory of symmetry-aware architecture selection, proving that generic symmetry defects lead to an all-or-nothing trade-off between fully equivariant and unconstrained networks, validated via synthetic & nonlinear controls.

Read Paper (OpenReview)
A network architecture is, in large part, a symmetry assumption. We study the trade-off between approximation error and estimation error under approximate symmetry in an exactly solvable model over cyclic group signals, concluding intermediate architectures are never strictly optimal under generic defects. Accepted for presentation at NeurIPS 2026 NeurReps Workshop (Proceedings Track).
